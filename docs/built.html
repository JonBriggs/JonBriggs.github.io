Built

Software made for the real work of schools. It's where the thinking gets tested.

I've written most of this software myself, with real help on design and on the migrations from one version to the next. Nearly every piece started the same way: a colleague had a problem that no product quite solved, not because the products were bad, but because a school is more specific than the average one any vendor builds for. Most of it is still running at Eastside Prep, which is the only review that counts.

When the pandemic closed campus, Eastside Prep held its graduation inside Minecraft. The video shows how it came together.

Avatari ↗

An LTI experiment in classroom gamification: students earned points, opened chests, and collected items to dress up an avatar — the whole wardrobe rendered as layered SVGs. Playful, and genuinely fun to use.

retired
Assignment Machine

Aggregated each student's assignments across all their classes and graphed them, reading special Canvas comment codes — M: for missing, INC: for incomplete. Retired when it wasn't used enough to justify the upkeep; a candidate to revive.
