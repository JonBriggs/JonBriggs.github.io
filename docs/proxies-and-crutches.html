Proxies and Crutches

Inspire · Spring 2024

On the stand-ins teaching runs on, and the supports it leans on — and what happens to both when AI makes the stand-in trivial to produce.

Teachers can't see understanding directly, so they rely on proxies: the test, the paper, the thesis statement, all assumed to track the learning underneath. It's the same move a machine-learning model makes when it overfits — memorizing the training data instead of learning the rule. Alongside the proxies are crutches: spellcheck, the calculator, the map — supports we come to lean on until we've forgotten how to walk without them.

With AI able to generate the proxy on demand, the essay argues that schools have to talk about how work gets done, not just assign it — and stop optimizing for the proxy, the grade, at the expense of the thinking it was only ever standing in for.
